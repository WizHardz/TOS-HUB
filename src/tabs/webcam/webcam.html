Camera

TOS HUB opens your webcam itself and sends the finished cam (picture, frame, shape and effects) to OBS as one source. Face effects and face follow work on every frame, perfectly in sync. Keep TOS HUB open while you stream.


          
Works with a GoPro HERO9 or newer (your HERO10 Black included), with no GoPro app needed. Switch it on and plug it in with a USB-C cable that carries data (the one that came with it is fine). Nothing to change on a HERO10: it has no USB setting, just plug in. Only a HERO9 has Preferences → Connections → USB Connection, which needs to be on GoPro Connect. The first time, Windows may ask if TOS HUB can use networks: click Allow, because that's how the camera sends its picture over USB. For long streams, GoPro suggests taking the battery out and running it on USB power, so it stays cooler.

          

            
            
            
            
          

          

          
Camera tips: falling asleep, running hot
          
If your camera has its own sleep timer (OBSBOT cameras have Auto Sleep in OBSBOT Center), turn it off or set it long. TOS HUB will also wake the camera if it freezes or goes black (it gives up after 3 tries in case it's overheating). Camera running hot? 720p at 30 fps is much easier on it, and TOS HUB rests it when OBS isn't showing it.

Full-screen effects
          
Effects can also fill your whole stream (right over your game), not just your cam. TOS HUB adds one see-through layer to OBS for this; it sits on top and stays empty until an effect plays.

          

          

          
Then switch the effect buttons under the preview to 🖥️ Whole screen, or pick a Whole screen action in the Triggers tab. Works for sparkles, zap, fire, frost, boom, hearts, confetti, magic circle, portal, meteor, bubbles and smoke.

        

        
My own gestures and poses
          

            
Pick the hand sign for each hand job. Any gesture works, including ⭐ your own (record them under My own hand gestures).

            

            

            
Switch things on and off with a gesture too:

            

          

          

            
Teach TOS HUB your own hand signs: 🤘 rock on, 🤙 call me, 👌 OK, 🤞 crossed fingers, anything. Hold it up to the camera and click Record; it learns it in about a second. Then use it in Triggers (Tracking → Hands) or Keybinds like any other gesture. Works with either hand.

            

            

            

          

          

            
Teach TOS HUB your own poses: a superhero landing, the floss, pointing at chat, anything with your arms. Strike the pose, click Record, hold it for a second. Use it in Triggers (Tracking → Body) or Keybinds. Needs your shoulders, elbows and hands in shot.

            

            

            

          

        

        
Triggers
          
What sets off what: hotkeys, your voice, your hands and face, mic sounds and Twitch. Each trigger reads When … → Do …; click one to change it. One trigger can do several things at once. To make TOS HUB press keys for you, use the Keybinds tab.

    Full-body tracking
          
          
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            
            
    
          
          
          
    
          
    Works best when the camera can see you from the waist up. Hold a pose for a moment to set it off. Body effects show on your real picture (with an avatar on, its arms copy yours instead). The glowing aura looks best with the background cut out, because it then shines from behind you. Your body never leaves this computer.

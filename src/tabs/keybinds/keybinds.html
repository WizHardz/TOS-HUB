⌨️ Keybinds

Make TOS HUB press keys for you, set off by your voice, a hand gesture, your face or body, a mic sound, Twitch or another key. The keys go to whatever is in front (your game, Discord, a soundboard), or straight to OBS as an OBS hotkey. Each card reads Press … ← when …; click one to change it.

Want a key on your keyboard to set off an effect or OBS action instead? That's a ⌨️ Hotkey in the Triggers tab. Pressing keys works in the desktop app on Windows; a few games with anti-cheat ignore keys from other programs, so test it in yours.

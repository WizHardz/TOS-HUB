First-time setup
  1. Connect OBS and your Duo on the Connections tab (it walks you through it).
  2. On the RØDECaster Duo, set each pad you want to use to the MIDI pad type. (Pads set to sounds or effects send nothing to this page.)
  3. Open this file in Chrome or Edge and click Allow when it asks to use MIDI devices.
  4. Click Quick setup and press your pads in order (or click any pad in the grid and press the matching pad on your Duo). Then click a pad and use + Add action to choose what it does.
  5. Keep this browser tab open while you stream (it can sit in the background). Use Backup now and then to save your pad setup to a file.

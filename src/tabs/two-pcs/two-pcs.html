🖥️ Two PCs

Stream from one PC and play on another. Install TOS HUB on both: the streaming PC runs everything here (OBS, Twitch, chat, webcam, pads); the gaming PC presses keys in your game, listens for your keybinds while you play, and can show the stream's effects over your game so you see what chat sees.

    Can't link? (Windows Firewall)

    The first time, Windows asks whether TOS HUB may use the network: tick Private networks and allow it. For the effects over your game, the gaming PC also talks straight to OBS on this PC, so OBS needs the same OK (OBS → Tools → WebSocket Server Settings must be on). Both PCs need to be on the same home network.
